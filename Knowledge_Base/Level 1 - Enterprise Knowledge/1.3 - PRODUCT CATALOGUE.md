# Product Catalogue

## Purpose

This document serves as the master catalogue of products and services offered by HDFC Bank.

It provides a structured view of product categories, customer segments, ownership domains, supported channels, and relationships to downstream product documentation.

This document does not contain detailed product specifications, eligibility criteria, pricing, fees, charges, or business rules. Those details are maintained within dedicated product knowledge documents.
