## Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of HDFC Bank as an organization. It serves as the foundational enterprise knowledge document for the Knowledge Engine and provides context for products, platforms, customer journeys, business rules, and AI-driven experiences.

# Organization Mission

HDFC Bank is one of India's leading private sector banks, offering a comprehensive portfolio of banking and financial services to retail, commercial, corporate, institutional, and NRI customers.

The bank focuses on delivering secure, customer-centric, technology-driven banking experiences while maintaining compliance with regulatory standards.
