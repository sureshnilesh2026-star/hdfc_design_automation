# Banking Terminology

## Purpose

This document establishes the standard banking terminology used across HDFC Bank.

It provides a single source of truth for banking concepts, abbreviations, acronyms, customer terminology, product terminology, payment systems, authentication methods, compliance terms, and regulatory bodies.

All enterprise documentation should reference these definitions rather than creating duplicate or conflicting terminology.

# Customer Terminology

| Term | Definition | Aliases |
|------|------------|----------|
| Customer | Individual or organization maintaining a banking relationship with HDFC Bank. | Client |
| Prospect | Potential customer who has not yet onboarded. | Lead Prospect |
| Lead | Potential customer showing interest in a banking product. | Sales Lead |
| Primary Holder | Main owner of an account or card. | Primary Applicant |
| Joint Holder | Additional account owner with shared ownership rights. | Joint Applicant |
| Nominee | Person designated to receive benefits after the account holder's death. | Beneficiary Nominee |
| Beneficiary | Recipient of transferred funds. | Payee |

# AI Context

## Purpose for AI Systems

This document is the canonical banking vocabulary for the Knowledge Engine.

AI systems should:

- Resolve abbreviations to their full forms.
- Use these definitions consistently across all interactions.
- Recognize aliases when retrieving knowledge.
- Avoid generating alternate definitions.
- Link users to downstream documentation for product-specific or journey-specific information.

Example:

User Input:
> "I forgot my MPIN."

AI Resolution:

- MPIN → Mobile Personal Identification Number
- Authentication Domain
- Mobile Banking
- Route to PIN Reset Journey
