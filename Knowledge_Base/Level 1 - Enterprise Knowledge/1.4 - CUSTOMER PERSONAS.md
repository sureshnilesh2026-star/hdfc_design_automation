# Customer Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the enterprise-wide customer persona framework used across HDFC Bank.

It establishes a standardized method for classifying customers based on relationship status, demographics, behavior patterns, financial characteristics, product affinity, and lifecycle stage.

This document serves as the foundational customer reference model for all business functions and should be referenced by downstream product, journey, marketing, servicing, analytics, and AI knowledge documents rather than duplicated.

---

# Customer Persona Framework

Customer personas provide a structured view of customer needs, preferences, behaviors, and relationship characteristics.

They enable business teams, workflow engines, and AI systems to understand customer context beyond raw demographic or transactional data.

Customer personas support:

* Customer acquisition
* Personalization
* Customer engagement
* Product recommendations
* Journey orchestration
* Customer servicing
* Cross-sell strategies
* Retention initiatives
* Customer experience optimization

---

# Why Customer Personas Matter

Customers with similar financial profiles may demonstrate significantly different banking behaviors, product preferences, and engagement patterns.

Persona-based segmentation enables:

* Personalized customer experiences
* Improved customer engagement
* Increased product adoption
* Better customer retention
* Enhanced marketing effectiveness
* Improved servicing outcomes
* Context-aware AI interactions
* Intelligent workflow automation

# Relationship Classification

## New-To-Bank (NTB)

A customer who currently has no active relationship with HDFC Bank.

### Characteristics

* No active account relationship
* No active loan relationship
* No active card relationship
* Limited internal customer history

### Business Objectives

* Customer acquisition
* Onboarding
* KYC completion
* Product education
* Relationship establishment

### Common Products

* Savings Account
* Credit Cards
* Personal Loans
* Digital Banking Services

---

## Existing-To-Bank (ETB)

A customer who already maintains one or more active relationships with HDFC Bank.

### Characteristics

* Existing customer profile
* Historical transaction records
* Known engagement patterns
* Existing product ownership

### Business Objectives

* Cross-sell
* Upsell
* Relationship expansion
* Retention

### Common Opportunities

* Additional Credit Cards
* Loans
* Investment Products
* Insurance Products
* Premium Banking Programs

---

# Relationship Personas

## Prospect

Potential customer with no active banking relationship.

### Typical Behavior

* Researches products online
* Compares multiple banks
* Responds to campaigns
* Evaluates product benefits

### Business Goal

Convert to active customer.

---

## Single Product Customer

Customer holding one active banking product.

### Examples

* Savings Account only
* Credit Card only
* Personal Loan only

### Business Goal

Expand product holdings.

---

## Multi-Product Customer

Customer holding multiple banking products.

### Characteristics

* Higher engagement
* Higher retention
* Stronger relationship value
* Increased profitability

### Business Goal

Increase customer lifetime value.

---

## Premium Relationship Customer

Customer enrolled in premium banking programs.

### Examples

* Preferred Banking
* Imperia Banking

### Characteristics

* Higher balances
* Higher transaction volumes
* Greater product penetration

### Business Goal

Relationship retention and growth.

# Demographic Personas

## Student Persona

### Profile

* Age: 18–25
* Limited income
* First banking relationship

### Typical Needs

* Savings Account
* Debit Card
* UPI Payments
* Digital Banking

### Preferred Channels

* Mobile App
* WhatsApp Banking
* Self-Service Channels

---

## Young Professional Persona

### Profile

* Age: 22–35
* Salaried
* Growing income

### Typical Needs

* Salary Account
* Credit Card
* Personal Loan
* Investments

### Banking Priorities

* Convenience
* Rewards
* Digital-first experiences

---

## Family Builder Persona

### Profile

* Age: 30–45
* Established career
* Family responsibilities

### Typical Needs

* Home Loan
* Credit Cards
* Insurance
* Wealth Planning

### Banking Priorities

* Security
* Family benefits
* Long-term financial planning

---

## Affluent Persona

### Profile

* High income
* Significant assets
* Premium banking requirements

### Typical Products

* Wealth Management
* Premium Credit Cards
* Investment Products

### Banking Priorities

* Exclusivity
* Advisory services
* Lifestyle benefits

---

## Senior Citizen Persona

### Profile

* Retirement stage
* Conservative financial behavior

### Typical Needs

* Fixed Deposits
* Pension Services
* Wealth Preservation

### Banking Priorities

* Trust
* Simplicity
* Assisted servicing

# Behavioral Personas

## Digital-First Customer

### Characteristics

* Primarily uses digital channels
* High self-service adoption
* Minimal branch interaction

### Preferred Channels

* Mobile Banking
* NetBanking
* Chatbots
* Digital Support

---

## Assisted Banking Customer

### Characteristics

* Requires guided interactions
* Prefers human assistance

### Preferred Channels

* Branch Banking
* Contact Center
* Relationship Managers

---

## Hybrid Customer

### Characteristics

* Uses both digital and assisted channels
* Switches channels based on journey complexity

### Typical Pattern

Research digitally and complete journeys with assistance.

# Persona Mapping Principles

Multiple personas may apply to the same customer simultaneously.

### Example

A customer may be classified as:

* Existing-To-Bank (ETB)
* Young Professional
* Digital-First Customer
* Rewards Seeker

Persona classifications are not mutually exclusive and may evolve throughout the customer lifecycle.

# AI Context

## Purpose for AI Systems

This document provides customer segmentation context for enterprise AI systems.

AI assistants may use persona information to:

* Personalize interactions
* Recommend products
* Improve journey orchestration
* Support servicing workflows
* Improve customer engagement

### Allowed Usage

* Context enrichment
* Personalization
* Recommendation support
* Journey optimization

### Restricted Usage

AI systems must not:

* Make eligibility decisions solely from personas
* Approve or reject applications
* Determine pricing
* Override compliance rules
* Replace risk assessments

Persona data should always be combined with eligibility, compliance, risk, and customer-consented data.
