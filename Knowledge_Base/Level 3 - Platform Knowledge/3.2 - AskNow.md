# AskNow

## Document Overview

AskNow is HDFC Bank's AI-powered conversational banking platform designed to enable customers to interact with banking products, services, information, and journeys through natural language conversations.

The platform serves as an intelligent experience layer within HDFC Bank's digital ecosystem, helping customers discover products, access information, complete servicing activities, and execute banking journeys without relying solely on traditional navigation-based interfaces.

Unlike conventional digital experiences where customers are expected to understand menus, navigation structures, and banking processes, AskNow focuses on understanding customer intent and guiding customers toward successful outcomes through conversational interactions.

AskNow acts as a bridge between customers, banking knowledge, journey libraries, platform capabilities, and underlying banking systems.

This document provides foundational knowledge required by AI systems, orchestration engines, conversational assistants, and retrieval systems to understand AskNow's purpose, operating principles, architecture, responsibilities, and role within the HDFC Bank Knowledge Registry.

- Journey invocation

AskNow should utilize available customer context to improve relevance and reduce unnecessary effort.

- Previous interactions
- Active journeys
- Customer preferences

Security, privacy, authentication, and customer protection must remain foundational considerations throughout all interactions.

The primary measure of success is customer resolution.

Successful interactions should help customers:

- Achieve objectives
- Complete journeys
- Obtain accurate information
- Resolve requests
- Move forward confidently

AskNow should deliver consistent experiences regardless of the channel through which the interaction occurs.

Channel differences may influence presentation but should not alter customer outcomes.

### Understand Before Acting

# 9. Intent Management Framework

## Overview

The Intent Management Framework defines how AskNow identifies, classifies, manages, and utilizes customer intent throughout a conversation.

Intent represents the customer's objective, goal, or desired outcome. Understanding intent is fundamental to enabling conversational banking experiences because it determines how AskNow retrieves knowledge, invokes journeys, generates responses, and guides customers toward resolution.

Intent serves as the primary decision-making signal within the AskNow platform.

---

## Purpose

The Intent Management Framework exists to ensure that AskNow consistently understands what a customer is trying to achieve before determining how the platform should respond.

The framework supports:

- Customer objective identification
- Conversation routing
- Knowledge retrieval
- Journey resolution
- Response generation
- Customer guidance

Without intent identification, AskNow cannot reliably determine the most appropriate action.

---

## Intent Definition

An intent represents the outcome a customer wishes to achieve through an interaction.

Intent reflects customer goals rather than customer language.

For example:

### Customer Utterance

> "I want a new card."

### Customer Intent

Product Acquisition

---

### Customer Utterance

> "My address has changed."

### Customer Intent

Address Update Request

---

### Customer Utterance

> "Show me my statement."

### Customer Intent

Statement Retrieval

---

The framework focuses on identifying intent regardless of how the customer expresses it.

---

## Intent Lifecycle

Intent management occurs throughout the conversation lifecycle.

```text
Customer Input
        ↓
Intent Detection
        ↓
Intent Classification
        ↓
Intent Validation
        ↓
Journey Resolution
        ↓
Intent Fulfilment
        ↓
Conversation Completion
```

Intent remains active until:

- The objective is achieved
- The customer abandons the interaction
- A new intent supersedes the existing intent

---

## Intent Categories

AskNow supports multiple intent categories.

These categories help determine the most appropriate conversational strategy.

### Informational Intents

Customers seek information or understanding.

Examples:

- Product information
- Eligibility information
- Feature explanations
- Policy information

Typical Outcome:

Knowledge delivery.

---

### Transactional Intents

Customers want to execute an action.

Examples:

- Service requests
- Updates
- Banking activities
- Submissions

Typical Outcome:

Action execution.

---

### Service Intents

Customers require assistance managing existing relationships.

Examples:

- Address changes
- Contact updates
- Card servicing
- Account servicing

Typical Outcome:

Journey invocation and completion.

---

### Discovery Intents

Customers seek recommendations, comparisons, or guidance.

Examples:

- Product discovery
- Product comparison
- Product recommendations

Typical Outcome:

Guided exploration.

---

### Navigational Intents

Customers need help locating capabilities or services.

Examples:

- Finding features
- Locating journeys
- Accessing services

Typical Outcome:

Guidance and redirection.

---

## Intent Resolution Principles

### Customer Objective First

Intent determination should prioritize customer goals over literal phrasing.

### Context Matters

Intent should be interpreted using available customer context.

### Clarification When Needed

Ambiguous intent should trigger clarification.

### No Assumptions

The platform should avoid guessing when multiple interpretations exist.

### Outcome Focused

Intent should be resolved in a manner that supports customer success.

---

## Intent Confidence

Intent identification may produce varying levels of confidence.

### High Confidence

The customer's objective is clear and unambiguous.

Example:

> "Update my address."

Action:

Proceed with journey resolution.

---

### Medium Confidence

The objective is likely but additional validation may be beneficial.

Example:

> "I moved recently."

Action:

Confirm customer intent.

---

### Low Confidence

The objective cannot be confidently determined.

Example:

> "I need help."

Action:

Initiate clarification.

---

## Intent Clarification

Clarification should occur when:

- Multiple intents are possible
- Required context is missing
- Customer objectives remain unclear
- Execution risk is high

Examples:

> "Would you like to update your communication address or billing address?"

> "Are you looking for information or would you like to submit a request?"

Clarification improves accuracy and reduces customer frustration.

---

## Intent Persistence

Intent should remain active throughout the conversation until:

- The objective is fulfilled
- The customer explicitly changes direction
- The interaction ends

Maintaining intent continuity helps avoid repetitive questioning and improves conversational flow.

---

## Intent Switching

Customers may change objectives during a conversation.

Example:

```text
Intent A
   ↓
Clarification
   ↓
Customer introduces Intent B
   ↓
Intent Reassessment
```

AskNow should:

- Detect intent changes
- Preserve relevant context
- Transition appropriately
- Avoid losing completed work

---

Determining what customers want to achieve.

### Supporting Journey Resolution

Identifying journeys capable of satisfying customer objectives.

### Enabling Response Planning

Providing context for response generation.

### Improving Customer Guidance

Helping customers progress toward successful outcomes.

## Success Indicators

Effective intent management results in:
